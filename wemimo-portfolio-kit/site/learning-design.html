Course

Write Your Way In: Academic Writing for Multilingual Learners

Learn to plan, draft, revise, and edit academic writing in English, using all the languages you know.

Who it's for
Multilingual learners writing in academic English
Length
4 modules, about 3 to 4 hours per week
Format
Online, with self-checks and writing tasks

Inside the course

  1. Module 1: The Workshop. The writing process, planning in any language, and beating the blank page
  2. Module 2: The Blueprint Room. Paragraphs, clear connections, and academic genres
  3. Module 3: The Library and Builder's Floor. Using sources in your own voice, AI as a writing partner, and building the essay
  4. Module 4: The Gallery. Types of feedback, revising with a Feedback Diary, and your final essay and showcase
