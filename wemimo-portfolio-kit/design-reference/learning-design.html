Learning Design

Lessons and materials built for real communication

I design lessons, activities, and handouts that get learners using English for real purposes. The lessons below are free to use in your classroom. Please credit the author.

Instructional Design Foundations · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

How I Design

Clear goalsEvery lesson starts with what learners will be able to do by the end.
Real tasksLearners debate, interview, and write emails, using language to get something done.
Feedback built inQuick checks at every stage show what learners know and what to review.

Lessons

B2 Grammar 120 min Adult ESL

Articles: the vs. No Article

Learners spot common article mistakes, learn three simple rules, practice with a worksheet, and then use the rules in a lively debate about education and money.

B2 Grammar and speaking 120 min Adult ESL

Talking About Plans: Present Progressive for the Future

Learners talk about their week, fix common errors in pairs, and interview a partner about their fixed plans in a Schedule Swap.

View slides Lesson plan Error correction Schedule Swap
Intermediate Listening and vocabulary 50 min Adult ESL

Listening for Ideas and Sound Patterns

Learners watch a short video on genetically modified foods three times, for the main idea, the details, and natural speech patterns, then share their views in Opinion Corners.

View slides Lesson plan Opinion Corners cards
B2 Workplace writing 50 min Adult ESL

Asking for a Raise by Email

Learners draw a job card, write a polite email to their boss using because, although, and unless, and then reply to a partner's email as the supervisor.
